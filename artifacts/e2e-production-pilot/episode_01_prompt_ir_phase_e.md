# Episode 01 PromptIR Phase E pilot

The provider-free pilot compiled the current structured storyboard snapshot
into 15 PromptIR v2 artifacts. Scene 1 contains 8 shots and Scene 2 contains 7
shots. Every artifact retains the stable `plan_shot_id`, structured semantic
references, authority lineage, generation policy, and payload fingerprint.

PromptIR qualification does not imply model generation readiness. Adapter
payloads are derived only after explicit model and capability checks. No
provider, LLM, image, or video call was made.

Machine-readable details are in
`episode_01_prompt_ir_phase_e.json`, `episode_01_generation_payload_phase_e.json`,
and `episode_01_phase_e_trace.json`.
